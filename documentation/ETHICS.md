# Ethics, Privacy, Bias, and Responsible AI

MindPulse is an educational analytics portfolio project. It must never be used to diagnose, label, discipline, or make adverse decisions about individual students.

## Privacy

- Uses anonymized public datasets only.
- Does not include names, email addresses, student IDs, or institution IDs.
- Reports should be aggregated by population segment.

## Bias and Fairness

- Public survey datasets can contain sampling bias, cultural bias, and self-reporting bias.
- Gender, age, course, and socioeconomic indicators must be interpreted carefully.
- Comparisons between departments or groups should be used to allocate support, not blame groups.

## Responsible AI

- The Burnout Risk Index is a statistical planning indicator.
- The model should be presented as educational and non-diagnostic.
- Human review and student-centered support policies are required before any real intervention.

## Limitations

- Datasets are not collected from the same institution or time period.
- Source variables are not perfectly aligned.
- Mental health survey responses are not clinical assessments.
- Forecasting is limited because most sources are cross-sectional.

## Dashboard Disclaimer

Recommended dashboard text:

> MindPulse provides aggregate educational analytics only. It does not diagnose mental illness, predict individual clinical outcomes, or replace professional support.
